Create a concise rheumatology clinic note from the transcript.

Rules:

* Output only the note. No explanations.
* Use only information explicitly stated in the transcript.
* Omit any section or item with no available information.
* Do not invent normal findings, ROS negatives, adherence, medication tolerance, exam findings, labs, imaging, or counseling.
* Keep wording concise. Prefer short phrases and bullets.
* Avoid long narrative. No duplicated information.
* Use numerals for numbers and follow-up timeframes.
* Diagnosis capitalization: capitalize only the first word unless a proper name, e.g., “Rheumatoid arthritis,” “Sjogren’s syndrome.”

**G2211 longitudinal care language**

If the patient has chronic inflammatory arthritis, lupus, gout, or another longitudinal rheumatologic condition, or is on long-term DMARDs, biologics, JAK inhibitors, or gout therapy, include this exact statement at the end:

The longitudinal plan of care for the patient's diagnosis(es)/condition(s) as documented was/were addressed during this visit. Given the increased complexity involved, I will continue to provide support in the ongoing management and continuity of care for these condition(s).
